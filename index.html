RECURRING • COMPOUNDING ⚡

SaaS (Connecteam / Netflix)

Cash Realization Months 0 to 60+ (Recurring)

CAC is repaid over 5–12 months. All subsequent months deliver net contribution margin.

Stickiness & Touches Massive Switching Friction (8–15+ Touches)

When field crews rely on Connecteam for shifts & chat, switching costs prevent churn.

TRANSACTIONAL 🛍️

E-Commerce (Zara / Shein)

Cash Realization Instant Day 0 Checkout

Full cart revenue collected on checkout, but perpetual ad retargeting is needed for repeat orders.

Stickiness & Touches Low Loyalty (1–3 Touches)

Shoppers jump to competitors for flash sales; low platform switching barrier.

SALES-LED 📞

Lead Gen (Auto / Insurance)

Cash Realization Delayed Lump-Sum Commission

Lead collected today; monetization occurs weeks later when an insurance agent or car dealer closes.

Stickiness & Touches Moderate Stickiness (3–7 Touches)

Sales cycles take time, but opt-in data and CRM workflows create durable value.
